Welcome to the immuneML Galaxy Portal!

immuneML is a platform for machine learning-based analysis and classification of adaptive immune receptors and repertoires (AIRR).

immuneML is a free, public, Internet accessible resource for use by anyone (see the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service, and your rights under the GDPR)

Please see the following tutorials to get started:

  • Introduction to Galaxy - the minimal Galaxy essentials needed for immuneML.
  • Quickstart tutorials - run a simple immuneML analysis.
  • YAML specification (advanced) - instructions to fully customize your immuneML analysis.
  • immuneml.uio.no - learn more about what immuneML can do.

If you have any questions or other requests, please email us at contact@immuneml.uio.no, or report bugs at bugreport@immuneml.uio.no.
